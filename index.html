✦ 12 Years Old · 7th Grader

Prashmit Mohapatra

"I spent months building this world." A student from Odisha, India, currently in the seventh grade at Delhi Public School, Prashmit wrote this book to explore creativity and build something of his own.

What started as an idea soon developed into a full-length novel shaped by curiosity, persistence, and a love for mysterious and atmospheric stories. If I can build a world, so can you.

"To anyone who believes that imagination has no limits—this story is for you. May it remind you that even the smallest idea can grow into something far greater."
